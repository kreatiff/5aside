## 2026-03-10 - Adding ARIA Labels to Icon-Only Buttons\n**Learning:** Found several icon-only buttons (like delete, remove, close) across multiple pages lacking `aria-label`s. This is a common pattern in the app's components, particularly in data tables and small action strips, making screen reader navigation difficult as they announce only "button".\n**Action:** Ensure all future icon-only buttons are implemented with a descriptive `aria-label` or `title` that serves as an accessible name. A standard pattern should be established for delete/remove actions.
